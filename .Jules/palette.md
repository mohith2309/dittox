
## $(date +%Y-%m-%d) - Add semantic labels to icon-only buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons without semantic labels are completely inaccessible to screen readers, meaning visually impaired users have no idea what the button does.
**Action:** Always require or strongly encourage `semanticLabel` or `tooltip` properties on custom icon button widgets, and apply them diligently (e.g., "Go back" for back chevron icons).
